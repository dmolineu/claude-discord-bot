# Privacy Policy — claude-discord-bot

Last updated: 21 September 2026

claude-discord-bot is a personal, single-user application. It is not offered to the
public and has no users other than its author.

## What it accesses

With the account owner's explicit OAuth consent, the app reads:

- **Gmail** (`gmail.readonly`) — message content and settings, read-only. The app
  cannot send, modify or delete mail.
- **Google Calendar** (`calendar.readonly`) — calendar and event details, read-only.
  The app cannot create, modify or delete events.

## What it does with that data

Data is fetched on demand, used to answer requests in a private Discord server
controlled by the author, and is not retained beyond the life of the request.

## What it does not do

- No data is sold, shared or transferred to any third party.
- No data is used for advertising, profiling or model training.
- No data is written to any database or persistent log.

## Retention and deletion

OAuth credentials are stored only on hardware controlled by the author. Access can
be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately
and permanently ends the app's access.
